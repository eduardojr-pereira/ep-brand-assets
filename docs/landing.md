# Landing integration

The existing builder accepts an explicit optional `landing_content` value of `landing/content.json`. When absent, existing technical landing fixtures and the guide retain their original contract. The portfolio owns its editorial content and media; `tools/landing.py` validates the explicit media allowlist and maps authorized catalog records into canonical component inputs. `templates/landing_content.html` composes Header/Footer through `templates/landing.html` and uses existing editorial, action, catalog, evidence, marquee, disclosure and social macros. `landing/layout.css` and `landing/behavior.js` are page-specific dependencies loaded only by the explicit landing build. They own layout, hero replay, scroll-driven CTA geometry and local contact illustration/validation, not preferences, global motion triggers or shared interactive behavior.

`components/web/assets.html` and `scripts/assets.js` support an optional complete theme × locale image matrix, in addition to their previous independent variants. The asset service resolves it from the canonical theme and locale. The navigation primitive supports explicitly localized documentation links, whose destinations are validated by `tools/routing.py`; no per-page preference controller is added. The builder copies only approved declared consumer media and catalog-referenced technology badges for this mode.

This integration candidate is local-only. No delivery service or actual legal policies are configured, and publication rights are not implied by local validation. Existing reports are not rebuilt or migrated. Before publication, confirm legal destinations, third-party rights and a separately approved delivery integration. The preview plan is intentionally not a deployment plan.
